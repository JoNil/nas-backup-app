Privacy policy

NAS backup is a private, single-user tool. Its only user is its owner.

  • It accesses only the Google Drive files it creates itself (the drive.file scope).
  • Those files are encrypted backups, stored in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • It collects no data from anyone, has no servers, uses no analytics, and shares nothing with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
